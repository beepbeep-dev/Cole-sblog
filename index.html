About me

Weird stuff that probably shouldn't work.

I'm Cole, I'm 20, and I run gamingrentals.store, a gaming rental business I spend most of my weekends building out. I like making weird stuff that probably shouldn't work. Right now that's Coolguys, a living slime-mold creature in a carved purple agar body that I sense and steer with light using an ESP32 (my current cultures are named Steve and Bob). I'm also building a rock ROM: binary scratched into a rock from my backyard, filled with graphite, and read by a microcontroller probe. Before that I got a cork version working, which I call “CD.”

I print my own models on a Bambu A1 Mini, game on a SteamOS rig, and keep a couple of salvaged MacBooks alive. On the side I'm looking into Google's bug bounty program, practicing lucid dreaming, and watching anime now and then. I've also got a dog named Cooper.
